Manual

RUN4 CTL is a firmware for the M-VAVE FM-1 with two modes:

* RUN4 mode turns the FM-1 into a hardware controller for the iOS app RUN4: the 27 keys, 14 buttons, 7 encoders and the MASTER knob are sent to the app over USB-MIDI or Bluetooth MIDI, and the app draws its pages and lights on the FM-1's 240 x 240 screen and LEDs. The FM-1 makes no sound in this mode. * Synth mode turns the FM-1 into a standalone six-operator FM synthesizer with effects, an arpeggiator and a 16-step sequencer. It also plays from MIDI in, so it works as a sound module for RUN4, TOLF or any MIDI source.

Switch between them with SEL + HOME. The FM-1 remembers the mode it was left in.

RUN4 CTL is an independent project. It is not made or supported by M-VAVE.

RUN4 mode

* Keys play notes (MIDI channel 1 by default) with velocity, whether or not the app is there. F3 is the lowest key; OCT- and OCT+ shift the keyboard by octaves (-3 to +3). * Buttons, encoders and MASTER are sent to RUN4, which decides what they do on the current page. * Screen: a header in the colour of the current RUN4 track, four tiles (name, value, bar), and a footer with the page dots, tempo, bar and beat, transport and octave. * LEDs show what RUN4 tells them to. Without the app, a held key lights itself. * Keys still play without the app. If the app is not there, the screen says NOT CONNECTED and the notes go on.

Synth mode

SEL + HOME enters it; the screen then shows the Synth pages in the same style as RUN4's: a header in the page colour (orange for sound, teal for effects, blue for arpeggiator and sequencer), four tiles with a value and a bar, and a footer with the page dots, tempo, bar and beat, transport and octave.

Sound. Eight voices (the oldest, released one is stolen first), Dexed's FM engine (Felucca's port) with the 32 DX7 algorithms. 8 factory voices (F1 to F8) and 32 user voices (U01 to U32) in two banks. A chain of drive, chorus, tempo delay and reverb (room, spring, hall).

The delay is cut at 371 ms: at slow tempi the long divisions are shortened to it.

Sequencer. 16 steps of 1/16 notes. PLAY starts and stops it; the footer counts bars and beats and the keys' LEDs show the steps (steps that are on are dim, the cursor and the playing step are lit). With REC on, a key played on the SEQ page writes its note into the cursor step and moves on. The arpeggiator turns held keys into an arpeggio and shares the tempo.

MIDI in. In Synth mode USB, Bluetooth and the TRS jack play the synth: notes, sustain pedal, pitch bend, all notes off, on the channel set in GLOBAL (OMNI by default). The notes of the keys are not sent anywhere in this mode.

DX7 patches. Send a DX7 32-voice bank (SysEx, 4104 bytes) or a single voice (163 bytes) to the FM-1 over USB or Bluetooth while it is in Synth mode, from a librarian or a DAW. A bank replaces the 32 user voices; a single voice goes into the current user slot or the first free one. The screen says IMPORTED 32 VOICES (or 1 VOICE). A bad checksum or a cut dump imports nothing. The voices are kept when the FM-1 is switched off.

Not like a DX7. The engine reads the patch parameters but has no DX7 operator switches, MIDI controllers other than those above, microtuning or portamento. The factory voices are Felucca's own, not a DX7 ROM.

Bluetooth

Bluetooth MIDI starts when the FM-1 is switched on. Connect from your computer or tablet (the FM-1 shows up as a Bluetooth MIDI device named like its USB name); no pairing is needed. In RUN4 mode the app talks to the FM-1 over Bluetooth exactly as over USB; in Synth mode the MIDI in and DX7 dumps work over it. A USB-only package (without Bluetooth) exists for those who want no radio. There is no switch for Bluetooth in the menus.

Install

You need a Chrome or Edge browser (Web MIDI) and a USB cable.

  1. Back up first. Installing replaces M-VAVE's firmware, including your presets. Save the presets you want to keep with M-VAVE's own tools before you start.
  2. Connect the FM-1 and open the installer page of this firmware (install/ in the site folder, published by the project owner). Follow its steps. The command-line installer in fm1-base (tools/fm1_install.py) does the same.
  3. After the install the FM-1 restarts and shows RUN4 CTL.

Going back to stock. Install M-VAVE's own firmware package (V15) with the same installer: it accepts M-VAVE's package. RUN4 CTL's own settings (SETUP page) and the user voices of Synth mode are not part of it and are simply gone afterwards.

Connect to RUN4

Plug the FM-1 into the iPad or iPhone (or connect over Bluetooth, or use a Mac session), open RUN4 and enable the FM-1 in its settings. The FM-1 sends HELLO every second until RUN4 answers; then the screen switches to the RUN4 page. Unplug and replug: it starts over.

Button map

The FM-1's controls keep the same meaning on every page. The fixed layer:

ControlUse
ALGORITHM encoder (track)choose the track

PLAY, RECtransport

SELSHIFT: hold it for the second function of the other buttons

The page buttons open the page of the same name: HOME, ENV, LFO, FX, EDIT, GLO, ARP, SEQ. What each page shows is decided by RUN4 and by the track you are on. Press HOME twice quickly for RUN4's mode list (Tape, Poly, Disc, Perform, TOLF): SELECT or PRESETS move, HOME or PLAY pick, any other page button or SAVE cancels.

SETUP page

This page lives in the firmware, not in RUN4. Hold SEL and press GLO to open it (it works with or without the app). K1 to K4 change the four tiles, any page button leaves it. Nothing you do on it is sent to RUN4 and the settings are kept when you power the FM-1 off.

TileValues
K1 VELOCITYSOFT, NORMAL, HARD, FIXED 100
K2 AFTERTOUCHOFF (default), POLY, CHANNEL
K3 LED LEVELOFF, LOW, HIGH (the dim LEDs)
K4 KEY CHANNELMIDI channel 1 to 16 (default 1)

Aftertouch is measured with the keys' own sensors. The velocity and pressure curves were set up without a device; if a setting feels too soft or too hard, pick another curve.

Help

* The keys do not play: check the USB cable, and that the host shows an input called RUN4 CTL FM-1. * The screen says NOT CONNECTED: RUN4 has not answered. Open RUN4 and enable the FM-1. * Everything about the wire protocol is in docs/PROTOCOL.md.
